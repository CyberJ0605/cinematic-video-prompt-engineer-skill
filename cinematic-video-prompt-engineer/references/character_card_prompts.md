# Principal Character Cards

Use only when writing principal/recurring character image prompts. Default to one text-designed four-view card rather than a portrait followed by a second card-generation step. Respect an explicit single-view request; a brief supporting role, a face-only shot or an existing satisfactory asset may use a single image instead. Do not invent a card for a direct-video-only task.

## Design before layout

Write the current character's visible identity and costume first: age, proportions, face/hair, useful distinguishing details, clothing construction/material/color and necessary accessories. Use only authorized design freedom. Then merge the layout below. With no image, say original text-designed character, not “based on the reference” or a fictitious image binding. With an already selected portrait, bind that actual image for identity instead of redesigning it. Do not require generating a single portrait first.

One card is one identity and one costume/state shown several times. Keep different principal identities and incompatible costume/injury states separate. The card is a foundation asset, not a dramatic first frame: inspect and confirm the actual face, side profile, clothing/back construction, anatomy and asymmetric details before dependent scene/relationship images. Approval of a prompt is not approval of the rendered card. User changes to confirmed design follow the existing affected-asset dependency check.

## Default layout to integrate in Chinese

```text
竖版四分区角色卡，细线分隔，左右等宽，上排约占1/3高度、下排约占2/3，不是等大四格。各格为同一人物、同一套服装的独立视图，不是多个角色或同场分身。
左上：正面头肩特写，平视、中性放松表情，完整保留头顶、发型与肩颈。
右上：同一人物自身左侧严格90°侧面头肩特写，鼻尖朝画面右方，不回眸、不用三分之二侧脸，完整保留头顶与后脑轮廓。
左下：放大的正面服装展示，放松A-pose、双臂微张、双手完整；从颈根到鞋底，整颗头部及所有头发完全出画，肩膀、双手和鞋底不裁切。只改变取景尺度，身体比例不变，视觉尺度明显大于右下全身图。
右下：严格背面放松A-pose，从头顶至鞋底完整入镜，不回头，展示后脑发型、服装背面和鞋履。
四格五官、发型、肤色、身体比例、服装结构与配饰一致；不对称细节固定在人物自身同一侧，不随视角镜像换边。未展示服装背面按既定结构简洁延续，不新增复杂设计。
统一中性灰无缝摄影棚背景，柔和侧向主光、克制补光与轻微轮廓光，曝光、白平衡和肤色一致。无文字、编号、剧情场景或时装摆拍。
```

Adapt to the established anatomy, age, costume and user constraints; do not force human A-pose or shoes onto incompatible designs. Card portrait ratio is a layout choice, not the video ratio. Do not add extra panels by habit.

## Face realism without identity drift

For live-action human cards, integrate concise observable texture: age/skin-appropriate pores, fine facial hair, small natural color variation and necessary expression lines; soft tonal transitions and natural restrained makeup. Do not exaggerate imperfections or add freckles, moles/scars, wrinkles or redness automatically. If selected as identity traits, keep their position/form consistent across views. Preserve the requested skin tone, not pale beauty-filter skin.

Keep eyes, face shape, nose/lips, hairline and side silhouette readable. Mild background blur is compatible; ornamental shallow focus must not hide identity detail. Neither 35mm nor a numeric aperture is a default requirement. Default to clean texture; subtle grain is optional only for an explicit compatible treatment and must not conceal details. Avoid waxy smoothing, greasy highlights, false whitening and sandpaper oversharpening; “no AI face” alone is not a specification. Preserve explicit non-photoreal art direction instead of forcing pores or film grain.

## Use after confirmation

Bind the card only for identity, hairstyle, proportions, costume/accessories and confirmed current state. Its panels, neutral pose, grey studio and separators are not video-scene content. Use a compatible single-view crop when needed; do not promise the full card is always the best model input. A crop is not a new-angle reconstruction. Check cross-view consistency rather than assuming layout proves identity; repair only failed fields. Keep the existing foundation approval and downstream-update rules.
