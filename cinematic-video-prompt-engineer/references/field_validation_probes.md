## Results and repair discipline

Record exact input, skill fingerprint, actual copy-ready prompt, model/settings/ratio/duration and selected references for each generated trial. Keep text, image, video visual and video audio results separate. Use timestamps for media defects; record listening coverage rather than inferring sound from frames. Keep failed outputs, not just the improved versions.

First classify the failure: ambiguous/contradictory prompt; fixture or submitted-version mismatch; execution not following a clear instruction; or insufficient evidence. One failed generation warrants diagnosis, not a universal rule. For repeatable or generalizable prompt defects, repair the smallest controlling instruction, rerun the affected probe and a relevant successful control. Change one major variable per comparison when feasible. Do not require repeats merely to fill a quota or spend generation credits without authorization.

Successful samples may be kept as examples without imposing their shot count, focal length, slow-motion style or choreography on other genres. This supplemental set supports targeted validation, not all-core or whole-skill maturity claims.
